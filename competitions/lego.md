## 📌 Competition Overview

**LEGO Robot Battle** was a robotics competition focused heavily on **mechanical design and robot-to-robot competition**.

The goal was to design a robot capable of effectively pushing and outmaneuvering opponents.

---

## 🎯 My Role

I worked on the robot's **mechanical design and movement system**.

I worked with:

- Core robot movement system
- Pushing mechanism design
- LEGO separator tool mounted at a specific angle
- Bluetooth-based remote control

---

## 🤖 Robot

The robot used a **LEGO Technic** drivetrain designed for stable movement and pushing.

A **LEGO separator tool** was mounted at a specific angle to create the robot's pushing mechanism and improve its ability to engage with opponents.

The robot was controlled through **Bluetooth communication** between a remote controller and the robot's control unit.

### Main Technologies

`LEGO Technic` `Mechanical Design` `Bluetooth` `Embedded Control`
