## 📌 Competition Overview

**WRO Future Engineers Thailand 2024** was my second year competing in the WRO Future Engineers category.

Our team continued developing an autonomous car-like robot for navigation and obstacle avoidance.

---

## ⚠️ Competition Challenge

During the second competition day, our **main microcontroller failed**, and we did not have a replacement available.

The failure significantly affected our remaining runs and ultimately resulted in a **4th-place finish**.

---

---

## 🤖 Robot

The robot used an **Arduino Mega**, a bigger version of Arduino Uno with more ports. But the Arduino Mega itself cannot utilize the motor and sensors to their fullest extent. That's where the motor driver and sensor shield come in.

The system combined camera and ultrasonic sensor data for **obstacle detection and navigation**, while **PID control** was used to maintain stable steering.

The robot design was based off of a car, adopting the "Parallel Steering System". This steering system allow the robot to steer with high mobility, allowing the robot to be able to U-Turn without taking much space.

### Main Technologies

`Arduino` `Computer Vision` `PID Control` `Ultrasonic Sensors` `Sensor Fusion`

---

## 💡 What I Learned

This experience reinforced the importance of:

- **Thorough hardware testing** before competition
- **Hardware redundancy and spare components**
- **Contingency planning** for critical failures
- Designing systems with **reliability in mind**

Despite the setback, the experience taught me an important lesson about the difference between a robot that works in testing and a system that is **reliable enough for competition**.
