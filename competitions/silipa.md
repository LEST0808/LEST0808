## 📌 Competition Overview

**Silapa 71 Intermediate Robotics** was a robotics competition focused on **light sensors and autonomous navigation**, consisting of two rounds: a standard track and a maze.

The standard round provided teams with a limited amount of time to prepare for a known track, while the maze round required the robot to navigate an **unknown environment autonomously**.

---

## 🎯 My Role

I worked on the robot's **control system and navigation algorithm**.

For the standard track, I developed a **modular control system** that allowed different behaviors to be quickly implemented for each section of the track.

For the maze round, I implemented a **block-based path exploration algorithm**. The robot checked available paths at each position and stored branching positions using an array. When it reached a dead end, it returned to a previously stored position and explored an alternative path.

---

## 🤖 Robot

The robot used **light sensors** to detect the track and make navigation decisions.

The control system was designed to support different behaviors for specific sections of the standard track, while the exploration algorithm allowed the robot to navigate the unknown maze and backtrack when necessary.
