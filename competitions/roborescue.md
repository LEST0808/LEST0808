## 📌 Competition Overview

**TPA Robo Rescue** is an autonomous robotics competition focused on **obstacle avoidance, navigation, and survivor search**.

The 2025 competition introduced **micro-ROS**, requiring teams to work with a new robotics software architecture.

Our team developed an autonomous robot capable of navigating an environment, avoiding obstacles, and completing the required rescue tasks.

---

## 🎯 My Role

My main responsibilities focused on the robot's **ROS parameters and camera system**.

I worked with:

- micro-ROS parameters

Before the competition, I independently studied **micro-ROS and its communication framework** to prepare for the qualification process.

---

## 🤖 Robot

The robot used an **ESP32** as the microcontroller, **LiDAR sensor** for environmental perception and localization, motor encoder to help ROS algorithm locate the current robot position better, and ESP32 camera for human detection using mediapipe.

### Main Technologies

`ESP32` `micro-ROS` `ROS 2` `LiDAR` `AMCL`

### 🔧 Technical Challenge
- Inconsistent LiDAR measurements
- Localization errors
- Unstable robot position estimate
- Navigation problems
