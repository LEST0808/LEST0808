## 📌 Competition Overview

**WRG Soccer Robot 1v1** is a robot soccer competition where autonomous robots compete in one-on-one matches.

I developed an **omnidirectional soccer robot** using three motors positioned **120° apart**, allowing it to move in multiple directions.

---

## 🎯 My Role

I worked on the robot's **mechanical design and vision-based ball tracking**.

I worked with:

- Three-wheel omnidirectional drive
- Camera-based orange ball tracking
- Robot chassis design in **Fusion 360**
- Internal weight compartment for increased pushing force
- Robot circuit system

---

## 🤖 Robot

The robot used a three-wheel omnidirectional drive system, allowing it to move laterally and diagonally without changing its orientation.

The camera tracked the **orange ball** and provided positional information to improve the robot's approach.

The chassis was designed with an **internal compartment for additional weight**, allowing the robot to increase its pushing force while keeping the weight contained within the chassis.

### Main Technologies

`Computer Vision` `Omnidirectional Drive` `Fusion 360` `Camera Tracking`
