## 📌 Competition Overview

**46 ICT Fast Line Tracing Competition** is a high-speed line-tracing competition for **Grade 10–12 students**.

I developed a high-speed line-tracing robot using an **eight-sensor array** and **PID control** to maintain stable tracking at high speeds.

---

## 🎯 My Role

I worked on the robot's **programming, control system, and mechanical design**.

I worked with:

- Eight-sensor array for line detection
- PID control for stable steering
- Higher-performance motors for increased speed
- Robot Design
- 3D-printed robot chassis

The motor upgrade was based on what I learned from the **previous PIM competition**, allowing the robot to maintain higher speeds while remaining stable.

---

## 🤖 Robot

The robot used an **eight-sensor array** to detect the line and **PID control** to continuously adjust its movement.

A 3D-printed chassis was designed around higher-performance motors, allowing the robot to achieve greater speed while maintaining stability during high-speed turns.
