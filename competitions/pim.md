## 📌 Competition Overview

**PIM Robotics Playground Line Tracing Open** was a high-speed line-tracing competition focused on **fast and stable autonomous navigation**.

I developed a high-speed line-tracing robot using an **eight-sensor array** and PID control.

---

## 🎯 My Role

I worked on the robot's **control system and mechanical design**.

I worked with:

- Eight-sensor array for line detection
- PID control for stable steering
- Robot frame design using **Fusion 360**
- Mechanical optimization to reduce drifting

---

## 🤖 Robot

The robot used an **eight-sensor array** combined with PID control to maintain accurate line tracking at high speeds.

The chassis was designed in **Fusion 360** with a low profile to keep the robot close to the ground and reduce drifting during high-speed movement.

Although the control system performed reliably, our motors were not fast enough compared with the fastest teams. This led me to research and upgrade the motors for my **next competition**.

### Main Technologies

`PID Control` `8-Sensor Array` `Fusion 360`

---

## 💡 What I Learned

The competition showed me that **control accuracy alone is not enough for high-speed robotics**. Hardware performance also plays an important role in achieving competitive speeds.

This experience directly influenced the motor upgrade used in my **46 ICT Fast Line Tracing Competition**, where I achieved a **Gold Medal**.
